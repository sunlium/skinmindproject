Treatment Options

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Evidence-based treatment that helps identify triggers and develop coping strategies.

Habit Reversal Training (HRT)

Focuses on recognizing picking urges and replacing the habit with an alternative behavior.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Helps individuals accept the disorder while committing to behavioral change.

Crisis Support

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741

Free, confidential crisis support via text message.

National Suicide Prevention Lifeline

1-800-273-8255

24/7 confidential support for emotional distress.
