Understanding Skin Picking Disorder

What is Dermatillomania?

Dermatillomania, also known as Excoriation Disorder or skin picking disorder, is a body-focused repetitive behavior (BFRB) characterized by recurrent skin picking that results in skin lesions.

What Causes It?

Often triggered by stress, anxiety, boredom, or other emotions. Can be performed unconsciously or during focused activities.

Who Does It Affect?

Affects people of all ages, though often starts in adolescence. More common in females but affects both genders.

Impact

Can result in significant scarring, infections, and emotional distress. Affects social, occupational, and personal functioning.
