# Skin Mind Project

Raising awareness about dermatillomania (skin picking disorder).

## About
This project is dedicated to educating people about dermatillomania and providing support resources.

## What is Dermatillomania?

Dermatillomania, also known as Excoriation Disorder or skin picking disorder, is a body-focused repetitive behavior (BFRB) characterized by:

- Recurrent skin picking that results in skin lesions
- Repeated attempts to decrease or stop the behavior
- The picking causes clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational, or other important areas of functioning

### Common Characteristics:
- Most often affects the face, but can occur on any part of the body
- May be triggered by stress, anxiety, boredom, or other emotions
- Often performed unconsciously or during focused activities
- Can result in significant scarring and infections
- Affects people of all ages, though often starts in adolescence

## Support Resources

### Organizations & Websites
- **TLC Foundation for BFRBs** - https://bfrb.org/
  - Largest non-profit dedicated to body-focused repetitive behaviors
  - Support groups, resources, and research information

- **International OCD Foundation** - https://iocdf.org/
  - Information about dermatillomania and related disorders
  - Provider directory and support groups

- **Psychology Today Therapist Finder** - https://www.psychologytoday.com/
  - Find therapists specializing in BFRBs and skin picking

### Treatment Options
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
- Habit Reversal Training (HRT)
- Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
- Professional mental health support
- Dermatological care for skin health

### Hotlines & Crisis Support
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **National Suicide Prevention Lifeline**: 1-800-273-8255

## Getting Started
If you or someone you know is struggling with dermatillomania, know that you're not alone. Reach out to the resources above for support and guidance.
