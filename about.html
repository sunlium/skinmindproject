About Skin Mind Project

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

The Skin Mind Project is dedicated to raising awareness about dermatillomania (skin picking disorder) and providing comprehensive resources to those affected by this condition. We believe that increased awareness and understanding can help reduce stigma and encourage people to seek the help they need.

Dermatillomania is a real, treatable condition that affects millions of people worldwide. Despite its prevalence, it remains largely misunderstood and underdiagnosed. Our project aims to change that.

What We Do

Educate: We provide accurate, evidence-based information about dermatillomania, its causes, and its impact on individuals and their families.

Connect: We link people struggling with skin picking disorder to professional resources, support groups, and proven treatment options.

Support: We foster a community where individuals feel understood and supported in their journey toward healing and recovery.

Why This Matters

Many people with dermatillomania feel alone and ashamed. They may not realize their behavior is a recognized medical condition with effective treatments. By raising awareness, we aim to:

  • Reduce stigma surrounding body-focused repetitive behaviors
  • Encourage early intervention and treatment-seeking
  • Help people understand they are not alone
  • Promote research and improved treatment options

If you or someone you know is struggling with dermatillomania, please know that help is available. Visit our Resources page to get started.
